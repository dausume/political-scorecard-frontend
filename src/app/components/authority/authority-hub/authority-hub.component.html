Group ↔ Polari Authority

A group of any kind can designate a Polari instance as its authoritative data source. A person holding authority on both the group and the instance defines the binding on the Polari side; activating it here defines the scorecard side. Once bound, the group asserts complex matters (like making a term available for a context) through that instance — every admission is checked by Keycloak-verified authority on both sides and recorded with its full evidence verdict.
