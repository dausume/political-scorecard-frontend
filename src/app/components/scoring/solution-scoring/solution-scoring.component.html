Solution Scoring

Develop, refine, and democratically choose the best solutions through expert analysis and community input

info Solutions are created and managed in the Polari Research Framework. Democratic processes for refining and choosing the best solutions occur by pulling solutions from Polari into static versions that are voted on here in Solution Scoring.

Expert-Level Phases

These phases are performed by domain experts in business, economics, technology, and policy.

engineering Phase 1: Requirements Development Foundation Layer

Experts develop the foundational requirements that solutions must address.

  • Business Processes - Define operational workflows and organizational needs
  • Economic Requirements & Dependencies - Identify costs, resources, and economic interdependencies
  • Technological Requirements & Dependencies - Specify technical capabilities and system integrations needed
 account_tree Phase 2: Logical Equivalence Assertions Abstraction Layer

Experts assert that specific Solutions are logically equivalent to abstract processes defined in Phase 1.

  • Map concrete solutions to abstract process requirements
  • Establish formal equivalence relationships
  • Enable comparison across different solution implementations
 fact_check Phase 3: Implications & Viability Analysis Layer

Experts, communities, and stakeholders assert implications and viability factors for Solutions.

  • Assess real-world implications of implementing solutions
  • Identify viability factors (feasibility, scalability, sustainability)
  • Gather input from affected communities and domain experts
 analytics Phase 4: WorldView Score Assertions Measurement Layer

Assert which WorldView Scores should be impacted by each Solution.

  • Assertions at the state level - overall impact measurements
  • Assertions at logic decision branches - determining applicable measures for different process paths
  • Define success metrics tied to worldview dimensions
 merge_type Phase 5: Solution Composition Integration Layer

Combine solutions with custom logic decision chains to create a "Solution of Solutions" that becomes a policy process.

  • Combine multiple solutions with custom logic decision chains
  • Create composite solutions that address complex policy needs
  • Apply directly to policy as a specific solution implementation
  • Generate rough draft processes for implementing policy segments
  • Tie success measurements from the beginning of policy implementation
