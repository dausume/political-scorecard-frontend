About Polari

Empowering communities with open source tools for decentralized, democratic, and accountable political and economic systems.

Our Mission

Polari's mission is to ensure that every person has access to the tools they need to build and participate in fully decentralized, democratic, and accountable political and economic systems—all of which are completely open source.

We believe communities should have the capability to strive for a healthy, environmentally friendly, and fully localized economy—one capable of producing even advanced technology using purely local resources and manufacturing. This self-sufficiency reduces the risk of monopolization, which stifles individual capability, creates corrupt hierarchies, and suppresses growth.

By providing these tools freely and openly, we aim to ensure that democratic values and self-sufficiency capabilities can persist even under conditions where hostile forces seek to enforce reliance and dependency.

Our Projects

Polari supports a suite of interconnected projects designed to provide comprehensive tools for community empowerment.

The Generalized Research Framework Open Source Research Infrastructure

A comprehensive framework for conducting, organizing, and sharing research in a structured and transparent way. Designed to support communities in building and maintaining their own knowledge bases while ensuring reproducibility and accessibility.

The framework provides tools for hypothesis tracking, data collection, analysis pipelines, and collaborative peer review—all built on open standards and open source technology.

The Political Scorecard Democratic Policy Evaluation

A platform for transparent, democratic evaluation of policies and solutions based on measurable outcomes and diverse worldviews. The Scorecard enables communities to define what matters to them and measure how well policies address those concerns.

Rather than relying on partisan rhetoric, the Scorecard transforms abstract debates into concrete, comparable metrics that bridge expert analysis with public participation.

The Isle-Mesh Resilient Mesh Network Infrastructure

A Linux-based, app-oriented system for deploying containerized web applications over mesh networks using Reticulum. Isle-Mesh uses Docker automation to make modern, self-hostable web applications accessible over secure, decentralized networks that communities control.

These "uncontrollable" but secure networks ensure that communities can operate resiliently—even under authoritarian regimes attempting suppression. Isle-Mesh guarantees continuity of democratic values and self-sufficiency capabilities when traditional infrastructure is compromised or controlled.

The Open Source Economic Baseline Democratizing Manufacturing

A collection of methods, hardware designs, and software projects aimed at making it easy for people to start businesses for even complex manufacturing at low costs while remaining generalizable. The baseline leverages open source 3D printing, CNC machining, and other fabrication technologies.

The foundation for an open source economy already exists through projects like:

  • Manufacturing: Open Source Ecology, Millennium Machines, Voron
  • Food Production: Food forestry, aquaculture systems
  • Software Tools: FreeCAD, Kiri:Moto, Mainsail

Why This Matters

Freedom from Monopolization

When communities depend on centralized systems for essential services, they become vulnerable to exploitation and control. Our tools ensure alternatives always exist.

Democratic Accountability

True democracy requires informed participation. Our platforms make it possible for communities to evaluate policies on their own terms, not just those defined by elites.

Environmental Sustainability

Localized production reduces transportation, enables responsible resource use, and puts environmental decisions in the hands of the communities who live with the consequences.

Resilience Under Pressure

Whether facing economic disruption, natural disaster, or political oppression, communities with self-sufficient capabilities can maintain their values and support their members.

Research informs Policy Evaluation

The Generalized Research Framework produces the evidence and analysis that feeds into the Political Scorecard. When communities evaluate policies, they draw on research conducted transparently and reproducibly within the framework.

Scorecard guides Economic Development

Policy evaluations from the Scorecard help communities prioritize which economic capabilities to develop. By understanding which solutions score well against community values, the Economic Baseline can focus on the most impactful manufacturing and production capabilities.

Isle-Mesh ensures all tools remain accessible

All Polari tools are designed to run on Isle-Mesh infrastructure. This means that even if traditional internet infrastructure is disrupted or controlled, communities can continue to access research, participate in democratic evaluation, and coordinate economic activity.
