Explore Scoring Methods

Three distinct scoring approaches serve different audiences and purposes in evaluating policies and solutions.

Policy Scoring

Draft, review, and score legislation. Legal professionals make assertions and associations between policy text and proposed solutions, while the public validates through engagement.

Worldview Elections

Participate in worldview elections to democratically define the meaning and accountability systems for issues. Create your worldview ballot by weighting terms according to your understanding of how issues should be measured and accounted for.

Solution Scoring

For experts in science, engineering, and economics to propose and vote on comparative flow charts of alternative solutions.
