# Competitors in the Political Scorecard

Competitors are groups and individuals who compete democratically to create solutions, create policy, compete
effectively in the market in accordance with community defined terms.

## Professional Competitors

Professional Groups, companies, and organizations competing to define professional or academic definitions for subjects and terms.

## Political Competitors

Political Groups and Individuals who assert that, based on particular sets of ground-truths, scores should be weighted in a particular way
when they are being applied to "how reality should be" according to their own world-view.

## Policy Competitors

Policy Competitors propose specific policy and tie/assert it's ties to particular 'Solutions' -> process analysis, and/or tying it
to particular 'Scores' weighted according to a particular worldview they assert is the one that should be adhered to.  Whether that be
their own worldview, the worldview of their political party, or the worldview of the people in general of a state or country over a particular
bar (majority, 60%, 75%, 95%+, consensus).  The policy competitors bring together a complete assertion of how exactly legislation should come
together with implementation processes and analysis of them and those process implications, as well as how performance of that policy itself
should be accounted for after being implemented to see if it did it's job or not.
